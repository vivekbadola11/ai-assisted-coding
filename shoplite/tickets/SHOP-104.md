# SHOP-104 · ₹999 orders are charged shipping

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Bug |
| Priority | Medium |
| Reporter | Customer Support (Neha) |
| Status | Open |
| Labels | pricing, shipping |

## Description

Our banner says "Free shipping on orders of ₹999 and above", but a customer with a
cart of exactly ₹999.00 was charged ₹49 shipping.

## Steps to reproduce

Cart: 1 × Cast Iron Tawa (₹999). No coupon. Shipping shows ₹49.

## Expected

Shipping is free at exactly ₹999 (see `docs/business-rules.md`).
